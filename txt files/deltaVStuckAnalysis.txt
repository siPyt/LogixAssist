DELTAV WON'T MAKE THE STILL "AVAILABLE" AFTER AN EXCESSIVE DRAIN ALARM
======================================================================
GROUND RULES (operator-stated facts - do not drop or reopen these):
  1. The ONLY condition that causes the problem is the EXCESSIVE DRAIN alarm.
  2. The ONLY fix is to RESTART THE FILL EM (equipment module) in DeltaV.
  Any theory or change must be consistent with both. Anything else (start
  request, temperature, other alarms, comms) is out of scope unless the
  operator says otherwise.
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Program: MECO9497 (Logix report 10/15/2026) + DeltaV module WFI-LVL-CTRL
Long version with all side notes: deltaVStuckAnalysis_full.txt

SCOPE (kept narrow on purpose)
------------------------------
Only this: an excessive-drain alarm happens, it is cleared and acknowledged on
the PLC/HMI, the PLC looks normal, but DeltaV does not let the still become
available, so the still runs and never fills storage. THE ONLY FIX is to
restart the fill EM (equipment module) in DeltaV. (Earlier notes said "restart
the chart"; the operator has clarified it is the fill EM.)
Not investigating unless evidence points there: the start request (starting
works), other alarm types, comms/heartbeat.


1. WHAT IS CONFIRMED (PLC)
--------------------------
- Excess drain = the still was NOT allowed to send to storage for 10 minutes
  (EXCESS_DSTLT_DRAIN_TIMER, MSS_CONTROL rung 19). It is the result of the
  storage delay not completing (conductivity/temp/enable), not a shutdown.
- It only drives the horn, the HMI and DELTA_V_READ[3].12
  (0 = alarm/retained, 1 = cleared). It does NOT gate DSTLT_STORAGE.
- The PLC sends to storage again as soon as the cause is gone AND the 60 s
  DSTLT_STORAGE_DELAY_TIMER finishes (DSTLT_STORAGE = DELTA_V_READ[0].7 = 1).
- Reset: ALARM_ACK_OIT + ALARM_RESET_OIT, and EXCESS_DSTLT_DELAY_TIMER.DN
  must be 0 (ALARMS rungs 34-35, INPUT_MAP 36-37).
- 11/06/2026 test: conductivity setpoint lowered -> real high-conductivity
  alarm -> real excess drain, no PLC changes. Every watched bit changed and
  returned after acknowledge, and DeltaV did NOT hang.
  => the PLC side works and the alarm by itself does not hang DeltaV.

2. WHAT IS INFERRED (NOT PROVEN)
--------------------------------
- DeltaV's "Still Available" (FROM_STILL OUT_D8 / STL_STL_AVAIL) is very
  likely DELTA_V_READ[0].7 (DSTLT_STORAGE), judging by the bit order. Confirm
  which wire goes to OUT_D8.
- The DeltaV fill sequence (WFI-LVL-CTRL START_FILL for N1/N3/N1BP) sends to
  drain (DIST WASTE) first, then sets the inlet valve to DIST STOR and
  EM_FILL_STATUS to "Filling". Its actions are P (pulse) = run once on step
  entry. The transition expressions were not visible in the photos (the
  photos are the IDLE state).
- WFI-LVL-CTRL writes EM_FILL_STATUS.CV := 'WFI_LVL_FILL:N1/N3/N1BP Filling' in
  its fill steps (photos). That is the hand-off to the fill EM (inference: the
  EM uses it). Because restarting the EM is what clears the hang, the hang is
  most likely IN THE EM (its state/step/hold) or in the hand-off between
  WFI-LVL-CTRL and the EM, not in the PLC. The EM's own logic has not been
  photographed yet.
- Working explanation: the excess-drain event leaves the EM in a state (hold,
  waiting step, failed/aborted, or stale fill status) that does not clear when
  Available returns. Restarting the EM re-initializes it. Unproven.
- Whether the event hangs it may also depend on which step the EM / chart was
  in when the alarm came in (operator's theory; today's test recovered).

3. WHAT TO CAPTURE THE NEXT TIME IT HANGS
-----------------------------------------
(Everything else can wait. This is what settles it.)
A. PLC: DELTA_V_READ[0].7 and DELTA_V_READ[3].12.
     READ[0].7 = 0 -> PLC still not allowing storage (not a DeltaV problem;
                      check DSTLT_STORAGE_DELAY_TIMER .ACC/.DN and
                      AAH_200_DELAY.DN / TAL_200_DELAY.DN).
     READ[0].7 = 1 -> PLC is sending to storage and DeltaV is not following.
                      Continue with B.
B. DeltaV, the FILL EM (this is where the answer most likely is):
     - the EM name and its state (Running / Holding / Held / Failed /
       Aborted / Idle, etc.) and the command/phase it is in
     - the active step of the EM's sequence and the transition NOT passing:
       its expression and live values (double-click the transition)
     - EM_FILL_STATUS.CV value while hung (compare to the healthy-fill value)
     - the EM's inputs from the still / WFI-LVL-CTRL, especially Available
       (STL_STL_AVAIL), and any "fail"/"hold" conditions
     - N1/N3/N1BP_WFI_IN_VLV REQ_SP and PV (DIST WASTE or DIST STOR)
     - the drain-back time condition
   And in WFI-LVL-CTRL: the active step of each START_FILL chart.
   Also record exactly what the "restart fill EM" action is (which command, on
   which screen: e.g. abort/stop then start, or reset/restart) and what the EM
   state is immediately before and after it.
C. Take the same screens during a normal, healthy fill. The difference between
   the two is the cause.
D. Before the next hang, also note which chart step was active when the
   excess-drain alarm came in (this tests the step-timing explanation).

Free source of the same information: DeltaV Event Chronicle / Process History
View for the last real hang (if configured for these modules). It may show the
active step and what changed, with timestamps, without touching the process.

4. DELTAV-SIDE FIX (ONLY AFTER SECTION 3 SHOWS WHERE THE EM IS STUCK)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: DeltaV does by itself what the operator's "restart the fill EM" does.
 1. Find what the EM is waiting on while hung and make that condition depend
    on the LEVEL of Available (STL_STL_AVAIL / READ[0].7) instead of an event
    or a timer that was started earlier and never re-armed.
 2. Automate the recovery: if a fill is requested, Available = 1, and the EM
    has not entered/progressed in its fill state for N minutes, issue the same
    restart command the operator uses (once), and raise an alarm/message so it
    is visible.
 3. If the EM goes to Hold/Failed during the event, add the logic that clears
    that state when the cause is gone (Available rises), instead of waiting
    for a manual restart.
 4. Clear retained flags/timers on the Available rising edge, not only at EM
    start.
This is a GMP/WFI system: change control, test with operations, no PLC changes
needed for this.

MECO9497 DELTAV COMMUNICATIONS

DeltaV sends a heartbeat bit to the PLC at DELTA_V_WRITE[0].0. The PLC copies
this bit to HEARTBEAT.

When HEARTBEAT is on continuously for one second, TIMER_BIT completes and
starts two timers. TIMER_OUT completes after five more seconds. When it
completes, the PLC turns on DELTA_V_READ[0].5. LOSS_COM_DISPLAY completes after
ten more seconds and turns on HEARTBEAT_AUX.

HEARTBEAT_AUX is used in the master-control logic. When it turns on, it
interrupts the master-control run path in both Manual and Auto. The
operator-interface logic also sets OIT_SCREEN_CONTROL to 99.

When HEARTBEAT turns off, the timers reset and the timeout indications turn
off.

This logic detects the heartbeat remaining on too long. It does not detect a
heartbeat that remains off, and DELTA_V_READ[0].5 is a timeout indication from
the PLC, not a changing PLC heartbeat.
